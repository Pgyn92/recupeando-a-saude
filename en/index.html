Reading the evidence

A single paper is not the same as the total evidence.

Study design, population, duration, dose, consistency and limitations all affect how strongly a result can be interpreted.

Question
What was actually tested?
Design
Trials, observational studies and reviews answer different questions.
Limits
Dose, adherence, population and measurement method can change interpretation.
Conclusion
Use only the degree of certainty the data support.
